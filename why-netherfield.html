Why Netherfield

Led by experience, built on trust

A seasoned team with deep expertise in real estate, construction, finance and design, delivering secure, high-value investments across Greece.

Our mission

Twelve years in the market, twelve projects delivered, and three offices in Greece, Dubai and Cairo, so that every client makes the right investment decision with confidence and clarity.

We guide individuals and families from around the world through every step of the process: property selection, legal support, relocation and, once the keys are handed over, rental management. One team stays with you from the first conversation to the residency card.

With a track record of property sales in London and Greece, we bring deep market knowledge to each client we serve. Our team specialises in supporting Egyptian investors and expatriates across the Middle East, with guidance that extends to interior design and long-term letting.

Founders & partners

The people behind every decision

Decades of experience across law, investment banking, corporate finance and design, brought to bear on every property we put in front of a client.

Mostafa El Shibini

Managing Partner

For more than thirty years Mostafa has worked across legal, financial and real estate consulting. As the leader of Netherfield Developments he focuses on high-end residential investments, with a specialism in Golden Visa routes in Greece and England.

He is also Managing Partner at Redcon for Real Estate and at the Egyptian International Consulting Group: cross-border experience that connects investors from the Middle East with opportunities in Europe.

Amr Abou El Seoud

Chairman & Non-Executive Director, Tejara Capital

A certified public accountant with more than two decades in investment banking, corporate governance and strategic leadership. Amr has served as Chairman and Senior Executive Officer at Tejara Capital and on the boards of Aston Martin Lagonda and Investment Dar.

He played a central role in the acquisition and later IPO of Aston Martin, and his expertise spans Sharia-compliant finance and cross-border investment.

Amr Helmy

Design · “the Godfather of Egyptian Design”

Since the 1980s Amr has redefined design through cultural revival and innovation, pioneering his “seven years ahead” method. He founded the Egyptian Designers Forum and the Talent Academy for young creatives.

His concepts include democratic design retail with Designy, Egypt's first innovation consultancy for businesses, and works such as “Cairo 2200” that fuse heritage with future-facing thinking.

Samer El Waziri

Finance Executive

Thirty years of corporate finance, strategic leadership and operational excellence, including his tenure as Chairman and Senior Executive Officer at Raya Holding, where he led restructuring and international expansion in the frozen-foods sector.

Samer holds a Bachelor of Commerce from Cairo University and a management diploma from Harvard Business School.
