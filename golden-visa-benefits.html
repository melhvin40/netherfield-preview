Frequently asked

Golden Visa questions, answered

The program

What is the Greece Golden Visa program?
Greece's Golden Visa grants renewable residency permits to non-EU nationals who invest in qualifying real estate, opening visa-free travel across the Schengen Area.
Does the Golden Visa lead to EU citizenship?
The Golden Visa grants long-term residency, not automatic citizenship. Citizenship may become possible later through separate naturalization requirements, including residency duration and language criteria.
Do I have to live in Greece to keep the residency?
The Greek program does not impose a minimum stay requirement to maintain residency, one reason it appeals to investors who travel or keep a base elsewhere.

Costs & investment

What is the minimum investment required?
Minimum thresholds vary by region and property type. Netherfield's listings start from €250,000. Speak with our team for the figure that applies to your target property.
What costs come on top of the purchase price?
Expect transfer tax, notary and land registry fees, legal fees and the application fees for each family member. We set out the full figure for your specific case before you commit.
Can the property be rented out?
Long-term letting is generally possible. Netherfield also offers rental management for owners who want the property to work while they are abroad.

Family

Can my family be included?
Yes. Spouses, dependent children, and in many cases parents of the main applicant can typically be included under one investment.
What about schooling for children?
The Athens Riviera has several bilingual and international schools. We introduce families to the options near each property as part of relocation support.

Process & ownership

How long does the process take?
Timelines vary by case, but many applicants complete property purchase and initial residency filing within a few months of engaging our team.
Do I need to travel to Greece?
Much of the purchase can be handled remotely through a power of attorney. Biometrics for the residency permit are taken in Greece, so at least one visit is normally required.
What happens when the permit expires?
The permit is renewable so long as the qualifying investment is retained. We handle renewals for clients who keep their property with us.
